𝗠𝗨𝗦𝗜𝗖 𝗣𝗟𝗔𝗬𝗘𝗥
• The music player is marked as being in beta when using `/play` or `-mb play`.
• Added playback-stall and stuck-track diagnostics to help identify playback problems.
• Prefix music commands share the same player and queue as slash commands.

𝗧𝗘𝗫𝗧 𝗖𝗢𝗠𝗠𝗔𝗡𝗗𝗦
• Sending `-mb` by itself directs users to `-mb commands`.
• Added `-mb sync` for administrators to synchronize slash commands.
• Added `-mb uptime`, `-mb update`, `-mb commands`, and `-mb say`.
• `-mb say` requires Manage Messages permission.

𝗠𝗢𝗩𝗜𝗘 𝗖𝗢𝗠𝗠𝗔𝗡𝗗𝗦
• Added `-mb movie list` to list local movie files.
• Added `-mb movie search <title>` to search titles and show provider page links.
• Added `-mb movie play <URL>` to post a movie page link in the configured channel.
• Added administrator command `-mb channel link <#channel or ID>` to configure the movie channel.
• Slash equivalents remain available: `/movie list`, `/movie search`, `/movie play`, and `/channel link`.

𝗦𝗜𝗠𝗣𝗟𝗜𝗙𝗜𝗘𝗗 𝗦𝗘𝗧𝗨𝗣

𝗨𝗣𝗗𝗔𝗧𝗘 𝗟𝗢𝗚
